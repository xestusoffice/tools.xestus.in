Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026 • Published by Xestus Technologies

1. Executive Summary & Zero-Server Guarantee

At Xestus OmniTools (an initiative of Xestus Technologies), we believe privacy is a fundamental human right. Unlike standard utility websites, our entire software architecture is engineered on client-side execution. When you compress an image, merge PDF contracts, format JSON records, or generate passwords, zero bytes are uploaded to our servers. All processing happens within your browser's local memory.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT store or log uploaded images, photos, or documents.
  • We do NOT save or read your generated passwords or cryptographic keys.
  • We do NOT inspect the contents of your PDFs or JSON payloads.
  • We do NOT sell, lease, or monetize your personal information to data brokers.

3. Cookies & Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense)

To maintain Xestus OmniTools as a free public utility without charging subscription fees, we display advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense.

Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users have the right to know what personal data is gathered. Because Xestus OmniTools operates on client-side sandboxing, we maintain zero user databases of your processed files.

5. Contact the Xestus Privacy Office

If you have questions or inquiries regarding our privacy standards, you can contact our privacy officer directly at: privacy@xestus.in.
