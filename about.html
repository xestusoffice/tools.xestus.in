The Xestus Mission

The modern internet is flooded with digital tasks that should be simple: compressing a photograph under 50KB for a job application or passport portal, merging two PDF documents, generating a permanent Wi-Fi QR code, or inspecting an API payload.

Yet, when ordinary people search for these solutions, they are greeted by predatory subscription walls, spam popups, forced account registrations, and dubious servers that harvest confidential files. Xestus created OmniTools to change this reality once and for all.
